AI Chatbot for Odoo

A floating assistant on every Odoo screen that answers from your company's own data: leave balances, HR policies, sales, invoices, stock and projects. Not from a generic model that stopped learning in 2024.

The problem it solves

A plain AI chat widget only knows what the model was trained on. Ask it "How many Casual Leave do I have left?" or "What is our leave policy?" and it can only guess or tell you to check with HR. This module fixes that by looking up the real answer in your Odoo database first, then letting the AI explain it in plain language.

What your team can ask

Key features

  • Company-data grounding. Personal HR data (leave balance, department, manager) and matching policy entries are added to the AI prompt before it answers.
  • Live database lookups. Using the AI provider's tool calling, the assistant can read Sales, CRM, Invoicing, Inventory, Project and HR records to answer questions about current business data.
  • Knowledge Base. HR or admins add policy text and keywords under AI Chatbot > Knowledge Base. No developer needed.
  • Works with your choice of AI. OpenAI-compatible APIs (OpenAI, Azure OpenAI, Groq, Mistral, DeepSeek, OpenRouter, Ollama and others), Google Gemini and Anthropic Claude. Switch from Settings.
  • Always one click away. A floating chat button on every backend screen.
  • Admin switches. Turn data grounding and live lookups on or off independently.

Security and privacy

  • Respects Odoo access rights. Every lookup runs as the person asking, with no elevated access. If a user cannot open Sales orders in Odoo, the chatbot cannot show them either.
  • Read-only. The assistant cannot create, change or delete records.
  • Fixed allow-list. Only specific models and fields (listed in the code) can be queried, and results are capped per request.
  • Data sent to your AI provider. The question and the relevant records it needs are sent to the provider you configure. Choose a provider that fits your company's data policy (for example a self-hosted model through Ollama).

Setup in 3 steps

  1. Install the module (it depends on Time Off).
  2. Go to Settings > General Settings > AI Chatbot, pick your provider, and enter the API key and model.
  3. Optional: add policy entries under AI Chatbot > Knowledge Base.

Requirements

  • Odoo 19 with the Time Off (hr_holidays) app.
  • An API key for your chosen AI provider (usage is billed by that provider, not by this module).
  • For live lookups, a model that supports tool/function calling. Sales, CRM, Invoicing, Inventory and Project are optional; the assistant only uses the apps you have installed.
  • Employees need a linked Employee record to get personal HR answers.

Good to know

  • Answers about general world knowledge still come from the AI model and may be out of date. Company answers come from your Odoo data.
  • If the data is not available, the assistant is instructed to say so rather than invent a number.
